Design Your Own Planet

Grade 9 STEAM, Beyond Our Planet, Lesson 6: The Planets in our Solar System (Extension Activity)

In this activity, you get to design a planet of your very own. Work through each section below: fill in your planet's birth certificate, decide what it looks like and what it is made of, and choose where it belongs. Your design stays on this page: nothing is sent anywhere. When you finish, print or copy your planet profile to keep it with your work.

Your design checklist

Work through the list. Each item ticks itself off as you go.

  • I filled in my planet's birth certificate
  • I coloured my planet and described its colours
  • I set my planet's average temperature
  • I decided what my planet is made of
  • I sized my planet next to Earth
  • I added my planet's moons, or said it has none
  • I decided whether my planet has rings
  • I wrote interesting facts about my planet
  • I compared my planet to the inner and outer planets
  • I placed my planet in our solar system or beyond
