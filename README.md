# Design Your Own Planet

A single-file planet designer for Grade 9 STEAM, Beyond Our Planet, Lesson 6: The Planets in our Solar System (Extension Activity). It replaces a Jamboard activity that no longer works.

Students design their own planet following the activity's own sections, in order:

- Fill in the planet's birth certificate: name, date of birth (how many years ago), galaxy of birth, and diameter across in kilometres.
- Paint the planet inside its circle and describe its colours.
- Set the average temperature: hot, cold, or in between, plus an average temperature.
- Decide what the planet is made of: rocky like Earth, gaseous like Jupiter, or a mix.
- Size the planet next to Earth with a slider and a live size comparison picture.
- Add moons, each with its own name (or say the planet has none).
- Decide on rings: how many (up to five, shown around the planet) and what they are like.
- Write interesting facts on sticky notes.
- Compare the planet to the inner planets and the outer planets.
- Place the planet in our solar system or beyond, and explain why.

A design checklist at the top ticks itself off as the student completes each part. When they are done, the app compiles everything into one printable planet profile, painting included, that they can print or copy into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
